## 1. Privacy Policy / 私隱政策

### English
**1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Cloud Ingestion**  
PauseWell operates exclusively as a client-side utility. We do not maintain user databases, utilize tracking pixels, employ third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics), or set advertising cookies.

**2. Storage of Personal Information**  
Any data you enter—including application pipelines, notes, daily habits, and scheduled interview dates—is stored solely in your local web browser's storage (`localStorage`) on your own device. This data never traverses the internet to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.

**3. Data Subject Rights & Control**  
You retain absolute sovereignty over your data:
* **Export**: You may export your entire data repository into a standard JSON file at any time via the "Backup / 備份" feature.
* **Deletion**: Clearing your browser cache/storage or manually deleting items within the interface permanently purges the records from your device immediately.
